# Test on your TV (about 5 minutes)

Phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi. Install the APK (allow "install unknown apps" for the app you open it from).

1. Open the app. Your TV should be listed. If not: Scan again, or "TV not listed?" and enter the TV IP.
2. Tap Connect. A 6-character code appears on the TV. Enter it, tap Pair. Expect "Connected".
3. D-pad: up/down/left/right move focus, centre selects. Back, Home, Menu work.
4. Touch mode on: swipe moves, tap selects, two-finger tap goes Back.
5. 123: type digits on the keypad (e.g. change channel), OK selects.
6. VOL +/-, Mute, CH +/-. The "Volume N" label should follow the TV volume.
7. Apps: tap each tile. Note which ones do not open. Long-press a tile to fix its package name.
8. Add custom app (a package) and a custom link (e.g. a YouTube video link). Tap them.
9. Open a search box on the TV (e.g. in YouTube). The Keyboard tab should open by itself. Type, backspace, Send.
10. Close the app and reopen it: it should reconnect without a new code. Switch TV off/on and check it reconnects.
11. Power button turns the TV off (and on if the TV supports it over the network).

Report anything that fails, with the step number.
